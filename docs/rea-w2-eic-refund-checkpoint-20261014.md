# Bounded original W-2 to EIC/refund checkpoint, 2026-10-14

The private REA reconstruction now follows raw current-year W-2 wage and withholding input through the original wage/AGI/ordinary-tax programs, EIC earned-income/qualification/AGI/final amount programs, a source-declared amount graph bridge and parent payment/refund programs. Root verified the immutable evidence files and independently reran all 20 selected cases.

For a synthetic 2025 single filer with no qualifying children, explicitly supplied age 40 and US-home facts, $10,000 W-2 wages and $1,000 withholding produce $649 EIC and $1,649 refund. This is a finite independently authored interpreter executing decoded original programs and their bounded bridge. It is not an execution of the licensed TurboTax application, a live native dispatcher or an original saved return.

The cases cover current income/age/home boundaries and native identity/partial-information paths. Missing SSN can still yield a partial displayed EIC amount in the selected source path. That does not establish employment-valid SSN, statutory credit eligibility or paper readiness. The app keeps those factual and preparation gates separate from arithmetic.

Raw DOB-to-age production, nonempty qualifying-child classification, investment-income production and its limit, current/prior/override invalidation, physical graph-to-field transfer and complete native scheduling remain open. Three REA workers continue those inputs, full file read/write and typed schema/value ingress. The app's formulas remain independently authored from public IRS TY2025 rules; this checkpoint does not ship vendor binaries, tables, program graphs or decompiler output.
