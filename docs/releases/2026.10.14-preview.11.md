# Tax Reborn #000002 — preview.11

**Child income now reaches the parent's actual return.** An eligible IRS TY2025 Form 8814 election calculates per-child interest/dividend/capital allocations, additional tax, parent AGI, qualified/capital tax rates, AMT comparison, NIIT, EIC investment-income and investment-interest deductions. Each child receives a separate interactive official IRS form. Required source annotations, the template's split decimal fields and the real Form 1040 line 16 checkbox are filled and checked after PDF serialization.

Use **My returns → Synthetic examples → Open child income example**. The example produces AGI $201,300, final tax $37,516 and refund $12,484. Set child taxable interest from $2,000 to $0: refund changes to $12,868. Restore $2,000: refund returns to $12,484. Save, reopen and export the 12-page official paper preparation packet.

Qualification requires documented identity/age/student/parent-election and income facts. This profile supports ordinary domestic income and ordinary capital distributions. Special 25%/28%/Section 1202 distributions and other excluded child situations remain blocked. The election is optional and can increase tax; the app does not automatically compare a separately filed child return.
